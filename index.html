How it works

Snacking, turned into something worth tracking.

Three steps, every time you try something new.

AT THE STORE

Scan it

Curious about a snack? Scan the barcode and see how your friends rated it, along with everyone else who has.

AFTER YOU TRY IT

Rank it

Compare it against snacks you've already rated to build a ranked list over time, with no rushed 10/10s, just an honest pecking order.

ANY TIME

Discover more

Browse what the community already loves, see where a snack was spotted in stores, and earn small achievements along the way.

Ratings that mean something

You don't just slap a 10/10 on everything.

Every new snack gets compared head-to-head against ones you've already tried, and you pick a favorite, so your ratings actually reflect how you really feel, not just a gut reaction in the aisle.

Friends' scores show up right alongside yours, so you can see who actually agrees with your taste and who's completely wrong about pickle-flavored chips.

Beyond your own list

See what's worth trying next.

Browse the snacks the whole community already loves, check where a specific snack has been spotted nearby, and unlock small achievements as you go, and snacking turns into a bit of a game without ever feeling like homework.

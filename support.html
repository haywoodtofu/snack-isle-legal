Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings → Delete Account in the app. This permanently removes your profile, ratings, and other content.

How do I report a snack, comment, or user?
Use the report option available on the content itself (look for the flag or "..." menu). Reports are private and reviewed by our team.

I found a bug or have a feature request.
Use Settings → Send Feedback in the app, or email us at the address above.

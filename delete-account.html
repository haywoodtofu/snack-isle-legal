Delete your account

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Snack Isle account and what happens to your data when you do.

How to delete your account

You can delete your account from inside the app at any time — you don't need to contact us first:

  1. Open Snack Isle and make sure you're signed in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm when prompted.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. There is no waiting period and no way to recover the account afterwards — if you sign up again later, it will be a new, empty account.

If you can't sign in and need your account deleted, email snackislesupport@gmail.com from the address associated with the account and we'll remove it for you.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your account itself — email address and password.
  • Your profile: username, display name, and profile photo.
  • Everything you posted: ratings, notes, comments, likes, and your head-to-head snack comparisons.
  • Your wishlist and your store sightings.
  • Your follows, in both directions, and your followers.
  • Your blocked-users list and any reports you filed.
  • Your achievements, unlocked cosmetics, and unlock posts.
  • Any feedback you sent us, and any snack edit, photo, or merge requests you submitted.
  • Your notifications, and notifications other people received about your activity.
  • Your push notification token, so notifications stop immediately.

What is kept

Two things intentionally remain, and neither stays linked to you:

  • Snacks you added to the catalogue. Snack Isle's catalogue is shared by everyone, so removing a snack would also remove other people's ratings of it. Snacks you contributed stay in the catalogue, but the record of who added them is erased — they are no longer associated with you or your account in any way.
  • Photos you uploaded. Images are served from public storage, so a photo may remain stored and reachable by anyone who already has its direct link. It will no longer appear anywhere in the app or be connected to your profile.

We do not keep your account data for any retention period after deletion — the items listed under "What is deleted" are removed at the moment you confirm, not queued for later.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove individual content. Inside the app you can delete any individual rating (along with its note and photo), any comment you posted, any store sighting you reported, and any snack on your wishlist — all without affecting the rest of your account. You can also change your profile photo and username at any time.
